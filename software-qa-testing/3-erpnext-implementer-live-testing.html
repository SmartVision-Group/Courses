Different Beast

You're testing a business process, not just a screen

An ERPNext implementation is mostly configuration — workflows, permissions, naming series, print formats — layered on shared code. A bug here is usually a misconfigured process, not a broken function. Test the end-to-end flow, not the field.

06 / 16
Inventory

Stock testing checkpoints

  • Stock Entry types — material transfer, issue, receipt — each moves the ledger correctly.
  • Valuation method — FIFO / Moving Average produces the cost you expect.
  • Reorder & warehouses — reorder levels trigger, multi-warehouse transfers reconcile.
07 / 16
Finance

Accounts testing checkpoints

  • GL entries — every transaction posts to the correct account and cost center.
  • Tax templates — correct rate applied per item, customer, and region.
  • Reconciliation — bank reconciliation and payment reconciliation tie out.

09 / 16
Access Control

Test as the role, not as Administrator

Log in as a Sales User, a Purchase Approver, an Accounts role — each one separately. Confirm they can do exactly what they should, and are blocked from exactly what they shouldn't. Most permission bugs never show up under an Admin login.

Ready To Use

Sample UAT checklist

  • Every core business flow tested end-to-end with real-looking data

  • Reports and print formats reviewed by the business owner
  • Migrated / seed data spot-checked for accuracy
  • Sign-off captured in writing, with the scenarios tested listed
12 / 16
Watch Out For

Common ERPNext testing pitfalls

Testing only as Administrator

Real users hit permission walls Admin never sees.

Skipping the reverse flow

Cancellations, returns, and credit notes need testing too.

Ignoring print & report output

A correct transaction with a wrong invoice PDF still fails UAT.
